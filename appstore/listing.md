## Description (max 4000)
Mesh+ lets you text people nearby through a LoRa mesh radio when there's no internet or cell service. Pair a Meshtastic® radio over Bluetooth, and your messages hop from radio to radio across the mesh, so you can stay in touch on a hike, at a festival or when the network is down.

Setting up takes about a minute: pick your radio, type the PIN from its screen, choose your region and a name, and you're ready to chat.

The public channel, private rooms and direct messages all live in one list with unread badges. You can share a room with a QR code or a link, join one by scanning its code, and mute the conversations you don't want notifications from. Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted by the radios.

Mesh+ stays connected in the background and lets you know about new messages.

Every radio setting comes with a short note on what it does and when you'd change it, from radio presets and hop limits to Bluetooth pairing, power saving and the screen.

You can keep all your radios in one place and switch between them from the top bar.

If you don't have a radio yet, you can try the built-in demo.

Mesh+ has no accounts, ads or analytics, and your messages stay on your phone. It's an independent open-source app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Meshtastic LLC. Meshtastic® is a registered trademark of Meshtastic LLC.
