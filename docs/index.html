Mesh+ GitHub

Message your friends when there's no signal

Mesh+ is a small, friendly Android app for Meshtastic radios. Pair your node over Bluetooth or plug it in with a USB-C cable, and you can chat in rooms and private messages across the mesh without internet or cell service anywhere along the way.

Download for Android Source on GitHub

Free and open source. Needs Android 12 or newer and a node running Meshtastic firmware 2.x.

Set up in a minute

Pick your node, type the PIN from its screen, choose your region and a name, and you're ready to chat.

Rooms and direct messages

The public channel, private rooms you share with a QR code or link, and end-to-end encrypted direct messages all live in one list with unread badges.

Settings that explain themselves

Every node setting has a short note on what it does and when you'd change it, from radio presets to Bluetooth pairing.

Firmware updates from your phone

nRF52 boards such as the Heltec T114 and RAK4631 update over a USB cable in about a minute, or over Bluetooth when you'd rather not plug in.

All your nodes in one place

Switch between your nodes from the top bar and see which board each one is, the firmware it runs and its battery voltage.

Small and private

The app is about 3 MB, has no accounts or analytics, and keeps your message history on your phone.

Installing

Mesh+ isn't on Google Play yet. Download the APK on your phone and open it; when Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. Updates install the same way, right over the previous version.
