Terms of Use

Last updated October 16, 2026

Mesh+ lets you send messages over Meshtastic radio meshes, where anyone with a radio nearby can join the public channel. By using Mesh+ you agree to these terms.

No objectionable content or abuse

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Don't use Mesh+ to send content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit or otherwise objectionable, and don't impersonate others or spam the mesh.

Blocking and reporting

You can block a radio, which hides everything it sends on your phone, and report any message to us. Both are in the menu you get by long-pressing a message, and blocking is also on each radio's entry in the node list. You can always email reports to support@borodutch.com. We read every report and act on it promptly. Mesh+ has no servers of its own and messages travel directly between radios, so blocking is the fastest way to stop seeing someone.

Your radio and the law

You're responsible for using your radio legally where you are, including the frequency region and transmit power you set.

No warranty

Mesh+ is free, open-source software provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Messages may be delayed or lost, so don't rely on the mesh alone in an emergency.

Questions? Email support@borodutch.com or open an issue at github.com/backmeupplz/meshtastic-plus. See also the privacy policy.
