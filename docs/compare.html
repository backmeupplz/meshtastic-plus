Mesh+ and the official Meshtastic app

How they differ, checked on October 27, 2026.

Mesh+ is a small, MIT-licensed Android app for Meshtastic radios, made to do one thing simply: chat with the people on your mesh. It talks to the same radios on Meshtastic firmware 2.x as the official Meshtastic app, made by the Meshtastic project, so you can switch between them at any time and keep using the official app for maps, telemetry and full device configuration.

Why Mesh+

  • Setup in a few steps: plug the radio in with USB-C (the phone powers it) or connect over Bluetooth, then pick your region and a name.
  • All your chats in one list: the public channel, private encrypted rooms you invite people to with a QR code or a link, and direct messages, with unread badges.
  • An SMS relay: when one phone on the mesh has a SIM card, people in a private room can send and receive regular text messages through it. It's in the APK linked below; the Google Play build leaves it out because Play doesn't allow its permissions.
  • Firmware updates for nRF52 radios over USB, with every version Meshtastic publishes for your board to choose from.
  • Every node setting explained in plain words, a demo mode for trying the app without a radio, and an app of about 3 MB that needs Android 12 or newer.
